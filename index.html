Leadership

How I lead.

Every team I have led got the same four things from me. They are not slogans; they are the routines that produced the results above.

01

Market focus and account prioritization

Reps cannot work every account. I build the territory and ideal-customer view first, so the team spends its time on the opportunities most likely to close and most valuable when they do. At Cogent that meant a prospect database spanning 28 countries, ranked before anyone picked up the phone.

02

Coaching and accountability

Sandler-trained and metrics-driven. Managers who have carried a bag build reps who close; I have done both, at top-10-percent levels, and I bring that floor to every team through weekly one-on-ones, deal reviews, and clear performance expectations.

03

Pipeline visibility and execution discipline

You cannot coach what you cannot see. Every team I run gets account and funnel intelligence before it gets a new quota, so forecast conversations are about specific deals and next steps rather than feelings.

04

Collaboration across sales, technical teams, and operations

Complex deals are won with engineering, product, and operations in the room. I have sold alongside sales engineers, led an account management team at a Salesforce consulting partner, and run field operations at Comcast, so I speak each group's language.

Applied AI

Applied AI for Commercial Execution.

I build the tools my teams use. Each one removes a specific bottleneck in the sales workflow, and each was built and deployed for working account managers rather than as a demo. Product screenshots are available on request, and I am glad to walk through the tools in conversation.

Project Atlas

Account intelligence engine
Business problem
Reps spent hours per account assembling firmographics, network footprint, hiring signals, and likely contacts before a first call.
What I built
An engine that takes a company URL or domain and returns a consolidated account brief with a recommended opening play.
In the workflow
Runs at the top of the funnel, before outreach, so pre-call research is a minutes-long step instead of a half-day.
Research in one step

Built to replace manual pre-call research. Time savings have not yet been formally measured, so no figure is claimed here.

Implementation notes

LLM orchestration over public web sources and internal data, with structured output that maps to the team's account plan template.

Python, LLM APIs, structured account research

Email Outreach Playbook

Custom GPT for sequenced, persona-aware outbound
Business problem
Outbound sequences were generic, and it took too many touches to earn a first meeting.
What I built
A custom GPT that drafts persona-specific sequences from the team's proven playbook, so every rep sends the message that has already worked.
In the workflow
Sits between prospect research and the first send; reps generate, edit, and send from their own accounts.

PeeringDB Prospecting Engine

Data pipeline for territory-wide account discovery
Business problem
Network-connectivity prospects were scattered across ASN records, websites, and facility data that no one had time to reconcile.
What I built
An automated pipeline that pulls ASN, website, and network-footprint data from PeeringDB and related sources into a deduplicated, territory-ready prospect database.
In the workflow
Feeds account prioritization for three Bay Area teams and the Regional, National, and Global coverage model.
13,800+ companies

Prospect records across 28 countries, ranked for the teams' coverage model.

Implementation notes

Scheduled Python jobs against the PeeringDB API with BGP and ASN enrichment, deduplication, and export to the team's working views.
